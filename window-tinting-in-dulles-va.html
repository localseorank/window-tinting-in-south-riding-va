Virginia Window Tint Regulations for Dulles Motorists

Adhering to Virginia Code § 46.2-1052 is essential for drivers operating in and around Dulles to ensure vehicles pass annual state safety inspections and avoid unnecessary traffic stops. The law specifies visible light transmission parameters based on vehicle type.

Passenger cars must allow at least 50% VLT on the front side windows and 35% VLT on the rear side windows and rear glass. Multi-purpose vehicles, including SUVs, trucks, and vans, must meet 50% VLT on front side windows, while rear windows may be tinted as dark as preferred.

The team at Speed & Tech Motoring offers a comprehensive selection of compliant ceramic film shades that provide maximum solar rejection within legal limits. We also provide guidance and support for drivers who carry approved medical tint authorizations.

Maintenance Guidelines for Lasting Clarity

Proper post-installation care will help your new window tint cure properly and maintain its clarity for the life of your vehicle. Keep your windows fully closed for 3 to 5 days following installation to ensure that the adhesive cures securely against the glass.

During the curing period, you may observe minor moisture bubbles or slight haze. This is an entirely normal part of the evaporation process as water escapes through the film, and it will resolve fully within several days.

When cleaning your tinted glass, use only ammonia-free foaming glass cleaners and plush, clean microfiber towels. Ammonia-based solutions can degrade the film's protective hard coat over time, while microfiber ensures a streak-free, scratch-free finish.
