Compliance with Virginia Window Tinting Legislation

Maintaining compliance with the Commonwealth of Virginia’s window tint laws is critical for all Potomac Falls motorists. Virginia Code § 46.2-1052 establishes clear standards for Visible Light Transmission (VLT) to ensure safety on public roadways.

Under Virginia law, passenger sedans and coupes are required to have at least 50% VLT on front side windows and 35% VLT on rear side windows and the back window. For SUVs, crossovers, and trucks, front side glass must meet 50% VLT, while rear windows may be tinted to any darkness level.

Our expert advisors at Speed & Tech Motoring ensure that you select a window tint package that balances your aesthetic preferences with complete legal compliance. We also support clients who possess authorized medical waivers for enhanced solar protection.

Aftercare Instructions for Long-Term Perfection

Following a few simple maintenance practices will ensure your newly tinted windows maintain their pristine condition and optical clarity for decades. Leave your windows rolled up for 3 to 5 days post-installation to allow the mounting adhesive to cure fully.

During the curing period, you may observe minor haziness or tiny moisture pockets beneath the film. This is a completely normal part of the drying process as water evaporates through the film, and it will disappear completely.

When cleaning your tinted glass, always use an ammonia-free glass cleaner and a plush microfiber towel. Harsh chemicals containing ammonia can weaken the film's protective hard coat over time, while microfiber ensures a scratch-free, crystal-clear view.
